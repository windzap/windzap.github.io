SEO Keyword Scanner

SEO Keyword Scanner is a small, private research tool run by Ken Wong. It helps plan search engine optimisation (SEO) for small Malaysian business websites.

What it does

  • Reads keyword planning data (average monthly searches, competition and top-of-page bid ranges) from the Google Ads API Keyword Planner, for Malaysia.
  • Compares that demand with a website's public search positions to estimate missed clicks and their traffic value.
  • Produces a private opportunity report. It does not create, change or pause any ads, campaigns, budgets or account settings.

Who can use it

The tool runs on the owner's own computer and signs in only with the owner's own Google account. It is not offered to the public.
